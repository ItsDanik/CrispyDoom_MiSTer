# CrispyDoom hybrid core for MiSTer (Doom, Doom II, Final Doom, expansions, Heretic, Hexen, Strife)

@hybrid/CLAUDE.md

## This core

- Name everywhere: `CrispyDoom` (the engine: core, folders `games/dnk/CrispyDoom/` and `logs/dnk/CrispyDoom/`, `_Other/dnk/dnk-CrispyDoom_*.rbf`, release), `crispydoom` (game submodule, binary in `build/`, log, lock). The games and their menu entries (`_Other/dnk-<title>.mgl`): `package/games.txt` (in the zip as `games/dnk/CrispyDoom/games.txt`, from which the database's build makes the launcher's catalogue).
- The game is the `crispydoom/` submodule, branch `mister`. Its MiSTer code is behind the `MISTER_HYBRID` build option.
- Build: `./build.sh` (game), `./core/build_core.sh` (FPGA), `./package.sh` (zip in `dist/`), `MISTER=root@<address> tools/deploy.sh`.
- Release: the zip in `releases/`. Players get it once it is published to the database, only when Danik asks: `hybrid/launcher/distribution.py publish ~/dnk_Distribution_MiSTer releases/CrispyDoom_YYYYMMDD.zip` (the rule in `hybrid/CLAUDE.md`).
- Try a build on the MiSTer without installing it: `MISTER=root@<address> tools/test_on_mister.sh <id>|list|nodata|log|stop` (the rule in `hybrid/CLAUDE.md`).
- Lines marked `GAME:` in the scripts and `TODO` in the READMEs are the places the template left for this game.

- Benchmarks under `tools/test_on_mister.sh` with a launcher older than version 7 must pause its daemon (`kill -STOP`, then `-CONT`): the test core `CrispyDoom_TEST` is no port it knows, so it does its "between games" work every ~15 seconds, bash bursts of 0.5-1s that made the game miss fields. - The game's clock is the field counter (`MiSTer_TimeUS()`, `i_timer.c`); once the core is gone it runs on by the system clock, or a wipe waiting for time hung at quit.
- The ticcmds are built by the same microseconds (`GetAdjustedTime()`, `d_loop.c`, all four games; Danik, 2026-10-22, "once a second it drops a frame" in gameplay, not in demos). Through `I_GetTimeMS()` a tic began there up to 1ms after `I_GetTime()`'s, which at 16,768µs a field happens in a field every 46 to 121 (about once a second): that tic's ticcmd was built a frame late and the view's turn (`localview`) jumped a tic ahead, then stood still. Every frame was shown in its own field, so neither `MISTER_HYBRID_PACE` nor the diagnostic report saw it. Found by logging each frame's `viewangle` while turning with `hybrid/tools/uinput_kbd.py` in `-warp 1 1`: 9 jumps and 7 holds in 7 seconds of turning before, none after.
- Video: the window's 8-bit surface is the screen buffer (`MiSTer_SetVideoMode()` in `i_video.c`); hires, widescreen and vsync are forced off. *Resolution* is OSD bits 50 (640 wide, `hiresx`) and 51 (200 lines: 320x240 is value 0, the default; `MiSTer_Option(MISTER_OSD_240)` inverts it). 640x200 / 640x240: `hiresx` 1 with `crispy->hires` 0, so every x doubling of Crispy's hires uses `hiresx` and every y one `crispy->hires`; `projectiony` and `pspriteyscale` are halved against the horizontal ones, the automap scales x by `AM_XSHIFT`, `V_FillFlat()` doubles texels across; patches go through `dx` / `dy` as they are. 240 lines (`SCREEN240`): the view fills 240 lines with a vertical projection 1.2 times the horizontal one (`projectiony`, `pspriteyscale` in `r_main.c` / `r_things.c`; walls, sprites, flats, sky, weapon; light levels from the horizontal scale, `R_LIGHTSCALE`; the floors' steps across scaled by `projectiony / projection` in `R_MapPlane()`, which Crispy works out from the vertical distance alone: without it the floor slid against the walls when turning), the 320x200 graphics unscaled over it through `V_SetYMode()` (`v_video.c`): centred for menus and full-screen pictures, at the HUD margins for the status bar and the HUD (upper half to the top margin, lower half to the bottom one). The status bar is drawn after the view every frame (`D_Display()`); with it shown the weapon is raised to stand over it as over the original's. Screen sizes below 10 are 10 in 320x240. Changes apply at once (`D_MiSTerResolution()`).
- Pad: Main_MiSTer presses the d-pad as well when the left stick is pushed far (a digital pad for the cores; seen on the MiSTer with `status.py` and a virtual pad, 2026-10-19: stick right gives joy1 bit 0 with analog 7f). The port ignores a d-pad direction the left stick is pushed in (`MISTER_STICK_PAD`, `i_joystick.c`), or the stick turned at full speed while it strafed (Danik's report), and in Heretic used the inventory.

- Music: `opl/opl_sdl.c` sends the OPL2 registers to the core's chip, timed to the sample in the audio callback; *FM Music: Software* (bit 26) switches to Nuked OPL3 at once, the chip loaded again from a shadow of its registers when switched back. `FM_VOLUME` 48 matches the software chip's loudness. The music is OPL2, 9 voices, whatever `snd_dmxoption` says (`i_oplmusic.c`, MISTER_HYBRID): Crispy's default `-opl3` (saved in players' config files) gave 18 voices, and as freed voices go to the back of the free list, about half the notes went to the OPL3's second bank, which the core's chip does not have (2026-10-20: Doom II's title music 180 notes on the chip in 25 s, 348 after).
