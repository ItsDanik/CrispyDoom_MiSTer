# CrispyDoom hybrid core for MiSTer (Doom, Doom II, Final Doom, expansions, Heretic, Hexen, Strife)

@hybrid/CLAUDE.md

## This core

- Name everywhere: `CrispyDoom` (the engine: core, folders `games/dnk/CrispyDoom/` and `logs/dnk/CrispyDoom/`, `_Other/dnk/dnk-CrispyDoom_*.rbf`, release), `crispydoom` (game submodule, binary in `build/`, log, lock). The games and their menu entries (`_Other/dnk-<title>.mgl`): `package/games.txt` (in the zip as `games/dnk/CrispyDoom/games.txt`, from which the database's build makes the launcher's catalogue).
- The game is the `crispydoom/` submodule, branch `mister`. Its MiSTer code is behind the `MISTER_HYBRID` build option.
- Build: `./build.sh` (game), `./core/build_core.sh` (FPGA), `./package.sh` (zip in `dist/`), `MISTER=root@<address> tools/deploy.sh`.
- Release: the zip in `releases/`. Players get it once it is published to the database, only when Danik asks: `hybrid/launcher/distribution.py publish ~/dnk_Distribution_MiSTer releases/CrispyDoom_YYYYMMDD.zip` (the rule in `hybrid/CLAUDE.md`).
- Try a build on the MiSTer without installing it: `MISTER=root@<address> tools/test_on_mister.sh <id>|list|nodata|log|stop` (the rule in `hybrid/CLAUDE.md`).
- Lines marked `GAME:` in the scripts and `TODO` in the READMEs are the places the template left for this game.

- Engine name `CrispyDoom` agreed with Danik (2026-10-19), after UZDoom was dropped for it. Games: Doom (shareware `doom1` without an entry), Doom II, TNT, Plutonia; TNT also loads `TNT31.WAD` when it is there (`MiSTer_GameArgs()`). Added 2026-10-19 on Danik's request, each with its own entry: SIGIL and SIGIL II (PWADs with episode 5 / 6 on The Ultimate Doom's DOOM.WAD, which `FindFiles()` checks has E4M1, else "not found"), No Rest for the Living and the Master Levels (Crispy's own `D_LoadNerveWad()` / `D_LoadMasterlevelsWad()`, Doom II "episodes" 2 and 3; under MiSTer only for their own entry; with the 20 WADs of the CD, which have no title graphics, the intermission draws the levels' names as text, the HUD font doubled and grey, `WI_masterName()` in `wi_stuff.c`, where upstream shows Doom II's), Heretic (shareware `heretic1` without an entry). New Game of an expansion goes straight to its skill menu (`M_NewGame()`); its saved games are `savegames/<id>/`. Hexen, Deathkings of the Dark Citadel (`hexdd`, `-file HEXDD.WAD`, saves `savegames/hexdd/`; Hexen's own entry gets `-nosideload`, or Crispy loads HEXDD by itself) and Strife (`STRIFE1.WAD` + `VOICES.WAD`, shareware `strife0` without an entry) added 2026-10-19 on Danik's request ("yes, add hexen and strife too"); menu titles chosen by me: Hexen, Deathkings of the Dark Citadel, Strife.
- Heretic, Hexen and Strife are the separate programs `crispy-heretic`, `-hexen`, `-strife`, packaged as `CrispyHeretic`, `CrispyHexen`, `CrispyStrife` next to `CrispyDoom`. The launch script always starts `CrispyDoom`, which picks the game (`MiSTer_GameArgs()`) and for these games `execv()`s their program in its place with its command line and `MISTER_CRISPY_GAME` / `MISTER_CRISPY_LISTED` (same PID, so the launch script's stop and exit codes work; quitting a listed game restarts `CrispyDoom`'s list). Tested on the MiSTer through the list with `hybrid/tools/uinput_kbd.py` (its first key is lost while Main_MiSTer picks the device up: start at 1500ms). Heretic's pad: the d-pad is inventory left / right, use artifact (down), fly up (up), L2 fly down (`mister_dpad_actions`, `i_joystick.c`). Its 240 lines and 640 modes are Doom's design in `src/heretic/` (status bar redrawn over the view every frame in `SB_Drawer()`, weapon raised by 79 rows of the original view, the automap's parchment filling 240 lines, pictures centred with black bands drawn after them because the end scroll draws every third tic only).
- Hexen and Strife (2026-10-19) follow Heretic's design in `src/hexen/`, `src/strife/`: weapon raised by 81 (Hexen) / 84 (Strife, not the targeter) rows of the original view, and in 240 lines clipped at the status bar's top (`barclip` in `R_DrawPlayerSprites()`), or its bottom showed below the bar in the HUD margin (Hexen's mace). Below the bar the 240-line view is the world (Strife's med kits on MAP02's floor: not a bug). Pad (`mister_dpad_actions` flags): Hexen inventory + fly (d-pad up flies up, L2 down), Strife inventory + objectives (d-pad up posts `key_mission` down / up, the popup while held; L2 runs); in both the Prev Weapon button jumps and Next Weapon alone goes round the weapons (`MISTER_DPAD_JUMP`: Danik, 2026-10-19, "it should be a face button", chose Prev Weapon's). The OSD's button names are the core's, shared by all games: the jump button is still called Prev Weapon there. Cheats: Hexen Give All NRA + LOCKSMITH + INDIANA (no puzzle items), Kill All BUTCHER, Next Level `G_Completed()` to the next map; Strife Give All PUMPUPP + BOOMSTIX (the Sigil kept) + JIMMY until the last key, message "Very Happy Ammo Added", Kill All STONECOLD, Next Level `rift<map+1>`; Buddha / Infinite Ammo / Notarget the port's own with their messages. Strife under MiSTer: ENDOOM off (`show_endoom`, it crashed at quit with no text screen), an empty save slot named "PLAYER n" when choosing it (`M_SaveSelect()`: a pad cannot type the character's name, and Enter on an empty one does nothing). Strife's title pages show about 35 new frames a second, levels 60.
- Frame pacing (hybrid rule): `DRAW_AHEAD` 1. Frames finish up to 16.3ms into the 16.8ms field without drawing ahead. 640 modes (2026-10-19), D_Display() timed: Plutonia demo1 640x200 0 against 121 (display 9.2ms avg, max 18.4), 640x240 67 (24 + 43) against 1964 (12.7ms avg, worst 300-frame average 15.9, max 24.3; 320x240 7.3, 320x200 5.4); Doom II demo2 640x240 0 against 156 (11.4ms), 640x200 0 against 1. 640x240 is not a steady 60 in the heaviest maps; the player's choice, documented. Heretic (2026-10-19, drawing ahead, frames shown twice + never): demo1 (3,000 frames) 320x200 0, 320x240 1 + 1, 640x200 2 + 2, 640x240 10 + 73; demo2 640x240 46 + 96 of 5,100. Hexen demo1 (4,500 frames, 2026-10-19): 320x200 10 + 10, 320x240 16 + 16, 640x200 33 + 97, 640x240 47 + 419 (3,900); with `-nosfx` 320x200 1 + 1: the pitch shift of every sound as it starts (`PitchShift()`, a float division per sample) took long sounds most of a frame; stepping instead (`i_sdlsound.c`, MiSTer only) gives 320x200 4 + 4, 320x240 4 + 4, 640x200 9 + 30, 640x240 20 + 282 (4,200). Strife demo1 (3,300): 320x200 0, 320x240 0, 640x200 2 + 2, 640x240 21 + 35 (before the pitch fix; Strife does not shift pitch). Benchmark helper: `bench5.sh` in the session scratchpad wrote the test CFG's byte 6 (bits 50 / 51) and ran `-playdemo` through `TEST_ENV`.
- Benchmarks under `tools/test_on_mister.sh` with a launcher older than version 7 must pause its daemon (`kill -STOP`, then `-CONT`): the test core `CrispyDoom_TEST` is no port it knows, so it does its "between games" work every ~15 seconds, bash bursts of 0.5-1s that made the game miss fields. Launcher 7 (`hybrid/`) leaves `*_TEST` set names alone. Players' games are not affected.
- The game's clock is the field counter (`MiSTer_TimeUS()`, `i_timer.c`); once the core is gone it runs on by the system clock, or a wipe waiting for time hung at quit.
- Video: the window's 8-bit surface is the screen buffer (`MiSTer_SetVideoMode()` in `i_video.c`); hires, widescreen and vsync are forced off. *Resolution* is OSD bits 50 (640 wide, `hiresx`) and 51 (200 lines: 320x240 is value 0, the default; `MiSTer_Option(MISTER_OSD_240)` inverts it). 640x200 / 640x240: `hiresx` 1 with `crispy->hires` 0, so every x doubling of Crispy's hires uses `hiresx` and every y one `crispy->hires`; `projectiony` and `pspriteyscale` are halved against the horizontal ones, the automap scales x by `AM_XSHIFT`, `V_FillFlat()` doubles texels across; patches go through `dx` / `dy` as they are. 240 lines (`SCREEN240`): the view fills 240 lines with a vertical projection 1.2 times the horizontal one (`projectiony`, `pspriteyscale` in `r_main.c` / `r_things.c`; walls, sprites, flats, sky, weapon; light levels from the horizontal scale, `R_LIGHTSCALE`; the floors' steps across scaled by `projectiony / projection` in `R_MapPlane()`, which Crispy works out from the vertical distance alone: without it the floor slid against the walls when turning), the 320x200 graphics unscaled over it through `V_SetYMode()` (`v_video.c`): centred for menus and full-screen pictures, at the HUD margins for the status bar and the HUD (upper half to the top margin, lower half to the bottom one). The status bar is drawn after the view every frame (`D_Display()`); with it shown the weapon is raised to stand over it as over the original's. Screen sizes below 10 are 10 in 320x240. Changes apply at once (`D_MiSTerResolution()`).
- Pad: Main_MiSTer presses the d-pad as well when the left stick is pushed far (a digital pad for the cores; seen on the MiSTer with `status.py` and a virtual pad, 2026-10-19: stick right gives joy1 bit 0 with analog 7f). The port ignores a d-pad direction the left stick is pushed in (`MISTER_STICK_PAD`, `i_joystick.c`), or the stick turned at full speed while it strafed (Danik's report), and in Heretic used the inventory.
- Music: `opl/opl_sdl.c` sends the OPL2 registers to the core's chip, timed to the sample in the audio callback; *FM Music: Software* (bit 26) switches to Nuked OPL3 at once, the chip loaded again from a shadow of its registers when switched back. `FM_VOLUME` 48 matches the software chip's loudness. The music is OPL2, 9 voices, whatever `snd_dmxoption` says (`i_oplmusic.c`, MISTER_HYBRID): Crispy's default `-opl3` (saved in players' config files) gave 18 voices, and as freed voices go to the back of the free list, about half the notes went to the OPL3's second bank, which the core's chip does not have (2026-10-20: Doom II's title music 180 notes on the chip in 25 s, 348 after).
- Cheats page: the OSD options type the cheat (`ST_MiSTerCheats()`), Buddha and Infinite Ammo are the port's own (`p_inter.c`, `p_pspr.c`), all only with `crispy->singleplayer`.
- The core's game list does take the keyboard's Up / Down (retested 2026-10-19; an earlier "does not move" was a test that pressed Down as many times as there were games, so the selection went round to the first again).
- SIGIL / SIGIL II on a DOOM.WAD without episode 4: their message says the DOOM.WAD has three episodes (`FindFiles()`), not "not found".
- Fixed in `hybrid/` for this core (committed and pushed there, 82cbb82): `MH_Close()` with the audio still on waits for `MH_AudioEnable(0)` (the audio thread crashed in unmapped memory when the game quit after another core was loaded).
- *Resolution* is status bits 50 and 51 with 320x240 its first value, the default (Danik, 2026-10-19, for this port only: Doom and Heretic start in 320x240; the other hybrid ports keep 320x200). It moved there from bits 24 and 25 so that every saved setting starts at the new default instead of meaning another mode in the new order: bits 24 and 25 stay unused, players' settings still hold the old values there.
